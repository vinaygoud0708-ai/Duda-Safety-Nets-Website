Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about our materials, installation, and warranties.

How much do safety nets cost in Bangalore?
Pricing starts from affordable factory rates per sq.ft depending on mesh size, twine thickness (1mm to 2.5mm), and material type (HDPE vs SS Invisible Grills). We offer a 100% free site measurement so you get an exact, transparent quote with no hidden charges.
What is HDPE and why is it best for safety nets?
High-Density Polyethylene (HDPE) is UV-treated, non-corrosive, waterproof, and heat resistant. It does not deteriorate under harsh Bangalore sun or heavy monsoon rain, offering up to 5-8 years of dependable lifespan.
Will safety nets block our balcony airflow or views?
Not at all! Our pigeon and balcony nets feature thin, high-tensile cords with optimal aperture (25mm or 30mm) that are virtually unnoticeable from inside and allow 100% natural breeze and daylight to enter freely.
How much weight can Children Safety Nets hold?
Our children safety nets are rigorously tested to support point loads exceeding 150 kg to 200 kg. We anchor them every 12 inches using heavy stainless steel expansion bolts.
How soon can you install the nets?
We provide same-day or next-day site visits across Bangalore. For standard residential balconies, installation is completed within 2 to 4 hours by our trained team.
Do you offer a warranty on safety nets?
Yes, our genuine HDPE safety nets and Invisible Grills carry comprehensive warranties covering manufacturing and UV durability, backed by prompt after-sales service.
